Personal Budget Calculator

A practical, client-side calculator to estimate monthly savings based on income and expenses. Validates inputs, handles common edge cases, and displays a clear result with helpful feedback.

Enter values and press Calculate to see your monthly savings.

Tips & Validation

  • All numbers must be non-negative; zero inputs are allowed but may result in zero savings.
  • Savings = Income - (Rent + Utilities + Food + Transport)
  • Projected savings = (Savings) * (Savings Rate / 100)
  • Handle edge cases like very low income or high expenses gracefully with clear messages.
